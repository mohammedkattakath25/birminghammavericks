Questionsbefore your first Sunday

Where do you play home games?

Old Halesonians Association Community Sports Centre, Wassell Grove Ln, Hagley, Stourbridge DY9 9JP.

What kind of cricket is it?

Hard-ball adult cricket. League matches in the West Midlands Sunday Border League are 40 overs a side, and we add weekday T20s and friendlies.

When is the season?

Matches run from April to September. We net during the season and indoors through the winter.

Do I need to be a certain standard?

No. We take adults of any standard who are committed to turning up and improving.

How does selection work?

Confirm your availability by Friday night for Sunday matches and tell us your preferred role. Regular availability gives you the best chance of selection.
